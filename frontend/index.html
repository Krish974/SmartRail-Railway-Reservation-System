Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the reservation process.

How does the AI Seat Recommender choose berths?

It evaluates factors including window view alignment, lower berth accessibility for elderly passengers, family grouping, and acoustic comfort by keeping you away from heavy coach vestibule foot traffic.

What happens when I select a seat?

Your chosen seat is temporarily locked in the database for 5 minutes. This prevents double booking while you fill out passenger details and complete demo payment.

How accurate is the PNR confirmation predictor?

Our Gradient Boosting ML model evaluates 2,000+ historical route booking trends, days remaining to journey, and class types to provide an explainable percentage estimate of ticket confirmation probability.
